## 1. Product vision
Create a fast, professional, trustworthy light-mode website that introduces Vishnupurikar Xerox, presents Ghansham Vima Seva Kendra and its verified LIC-related services, introduces the owner, displays real business photographs, and credits Khan Gulamgous as the website developer.

The site is an informational and enquiry website. It is not an online store, policy sales platform, customer portal, or insurance transaction system.

## 2. Goals
- Help visitors understand the available printing and document services.
- Make phone, WhatsApp, and location information easy to find.
- Present the insurance service information accurately and responsibly.
- Tell the owner's business story using approved content and photographs.
- Give the developer a useful, honest portfolio case study.
- Support English and Marathi across all public pages.
- Work well on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- Load quickly and remain simple to maintain.

## 3. Audiences
1. Local residents seeking photocopying, printing, binding, lamination, or related services.
2. Visitors seeking information about Ghansham Vima Seva Kendra.
3. People who want to contact the business or find its location.
4. Potential clients reviewing Khan Gulamgous's website-development work.
